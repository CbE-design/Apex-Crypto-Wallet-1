# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public authentication entry page (`/login`)
- Public legal and compliance pages (`/legal/**`)
- Public utility / informational routes such as `/coming-soon`
- Global crawlability files and shared metadata that affect public pages

## Out of scope
- Authenticated wallet application routes (`/`, `/wallets`, `/my-wallets`, `/send-receive`, `/swap`, `/cash-out`, `/settings`, `/ai-assistant`, `/explorer/**`)
- Admin routes (`/admin/**`)
- API routes except where they directly implement crawlability assets such as robots or sitemap

## Target audience
- Existing or prospective Apex Wallet users searching for the product, login access, and compliance information
- South Africa-based users evaluating crypto wallet compliance, privacy, AML, and risk policies

## Primary keywords
- Unknown — likely branded and compliance-led queries such as "Apex Wallet login", "Apex Wallet terms", "Apex Wallet privacy policy", and South Africa crypto compliance terms.
